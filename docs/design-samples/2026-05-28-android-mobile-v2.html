The IDE,
at arm's reach.

An Android client for the ForgeBoard development kit. Same loop as the desktop — write, compile, flash, observe — rebuilt for one thumb, one cable, one board on the bench.

§ I

Directive

Intent · Constraints · Voice
01VOICE
02SCOPE
03FORM

An Arduino IDE for the phone is a strange thing to want. Cables are for desks; phones are for elsewhere. Which is the whole point. The bench is wherever the board is — a vehicle bay, a hangar floor, the back of a Land Cruiser. The IDE follows.

This document fixes the visual direction of the Android client: the form the screens take, the typography that carries them, the single warm signal we permit through the cool dark of the chassis, and the moments we let breathe. Everything else is plumbing.

A.01RESTRAINT — one accent, one moment, one verb per screen.
A.02TACTILITY — touch targets ≥ 44 px. No exceptions, including in serial.
A.03CONTINUITY — the desktop tokens travel; we retune, we do not redesign.
A.04CANDOUR — surface what the board is doing. Lying to the operator is the only sin.
