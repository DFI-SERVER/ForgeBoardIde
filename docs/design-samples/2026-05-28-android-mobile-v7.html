// 0002

The brief.

Intent · voice · constraint

An Arduino IDE for the phone is a strange thing to want. Cables are for desks; phones are for elsewhere — which is the point. The bench is wherever the board is. A vehicle bay, a hangar floor, the back of a Land Cruiser on the perimeter. The IDE follows.

The brief is small. Five surfaces, one verb each. Sketch. Edit. Probe. Pick. Observe. The trick is the same as it was on the desktop: keep the chassis quiet so the operator can hear the board.

The form is borrowed from two strangers. From editorial publishing, the gravity of a real serif at display size — Newsreader, set generously. From hardware product design — Nothing's discipline — the dot grid, the heavy numerics, the calm restraint.

The chassis is warm graphite. The ink is warm cream. No other colour at all. Status is communicated through inversion — a cream block on dark, never a hue. Add a colour, and you must defend it against four other readings on the same screen. Don't.

Sketches,
at rest.

The landing surface. Live board chip with a target reticle, recent work below in grouped cards, the verb sitting as a pill at the foot — same convention as Claude's "New session", different ink.

01
Target reticle, not dot

02
Sketches keyed by refdes (SK1, SK2…)
Sketches carry reference designators like silkscreen on the PCB itself. The tool speaks the language of its domain.
03
"Flashed now" reads in cream
The brightest tone of the warm palette marks "this is what's currently running on the board". Status by typographic weight and tone, not by hue.

The primary surface. Pin literals matching the connected board's pinmap pick up a cream hairline — tap one to open Probe. The active line inverts to cream block. Flash is a cream pill at the foot — the only filled surface on the screen.

Cream block, dark text. Replaces every selection ring the desktop used. One language of "this is current".

04 Boards · select transport

Pick a
board.

USB and WiFi share one surface, grouped in soft cards with hairline rules between rows. The selected device inverts — cream block, dark text. No checkmark, no badge. Identifiers stay in mono so the operator sees the real address.

01
Group cards, not floating rows
Devices live inside soft rounded cards grouped by transport. Internal dividers are hairlines, not chrome — keeps the surface quiet.
02
Selection · full row inversion
The whole row becomes the indicator. No checkmark to track. Echo of the same inversion language used on the editor's active line.
05 Monitor · live serial

Listen,
then write.

Serial telemetry inside a soft card. The "LIVE" pill is cream-bordered, pulsing — alive by opacity. Levels carry weight, not colour: INF regular, DBG muted, WRN heavier. Reading is primary; sending is at the foot.

01
Level by typographic weight
INF regular, DBG muted, WRN heavier weight. The level is encoded in the typeface itself.
02
"LIVE" breathes by opacity
The single screaming colour in the whole app sits exactly where it should — on the indicator that the link to the board is live, right now.
